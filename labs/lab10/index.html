Lab 10: Interactive Physics

Launch, Fall, and Bounce

How do gravity and bounce affect a launched ball? Launch a ball, compare its motion with the predicted path, and explore what happens when it hits the walls.

Try it: Choose the ball-box scene and “Launch a ball.” Set downward gravity to 2 m/s², then press in an empty part of the box, pull backward, and release. Change restitution to compare nearly elastic bounces with bounces that lose kinetic energy.

The dashed preview predicts free flight under gravity. It ends at the box boundary and does not predict collisions with other balls. Launching and dragging add energy, so energy changes during interaction are not conservation tests.

Physics visuals

Zoom changes the view around its center; zooming in may hide bodies near the edges. Reset view restores 1× zoom. Arrow size magnifies both arrow types without changing the physics. Arrow lengths remain capped.

Velocity arrows show motion direction and relative speed. Force arrows show the most recently calculated continuous force; collision impulses are not included. Long arrows are capped. The energy graph shows recent total mechanical energy, including changes caused by interaction.

Keyboard: Tab to the canvas. In Grab mode, N grabs the next movable body; arrow keys move its spring target; G releases it. In Launch mode, Enter begins aiming; arrow keys adjust the pull; Shift plus arrow keys moves the launch position; Enter launches. Escape cancels either interaction. Space pauses or resumes; period advances one step.

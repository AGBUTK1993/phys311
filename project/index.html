Gravity and restitution apply to the ball box. Spring stiffness applies to the spring chain. Changing gravity or stiffness starts a new energy comparison.

Zoom changes the view around its center; zooming in may hide bodies near the edges. Reset view restores 1× zoom. Arrow size magnifies both arrow types without changing the physics. Arrow lengths remain capped.

Velocity arrows show motion direction and relative speed. Force arrows show the most recently calculated continuous force; collision impulses are not included. Long arrows are capped. The energy graph shows recent total mechanical energy, including changes caused by interaction.

Instructions

In the Burial Shaft, move the support slider to guide the suspended artifact. Tab to the slider and use the arrow keys for keyboard control.

Keep the entire artifact inside the delivery zone below 0.2 m/s for one continuous simulated second. Moving the support can add or remove energy; damping gradually reduces the swing.

Conservative mode fixes the support, removes damping, and disables delivery completion for physics testing.

Other physics scenes retain their own controls and question-mark help buttons.
